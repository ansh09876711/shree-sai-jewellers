The Beginning

A Legacy Built on
Trust & Craftsmanship

Founded in 1984 by visionary artisan Shri Sai Ratan Das, our journey began in a modest workshop in the heart of Indore's jewellery district. What started as a small family craft has grown into one of India's most trusted fine jewellery houses.

For over 40 years, we have been the custodians of traditional Indian jewellery artistry — from intricate Kundan and Polki work to contemporary diamond solitaires. Every piece that leaves our atelier is a labour of love, precision, and uncompromising quality.
